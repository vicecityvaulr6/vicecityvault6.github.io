vicecityvault6 clipper

A personal desktop tool that turns gaming videos into short vertical clips, credits the original creator, and posts the clips its owner approves to the owner's own TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube accounts.

  • Owner's accounts onlyPosts only to accounts the owner connects through each platform's official login.
  • Every clip approvedA person watches and approves each clip and caption before anything is posted.
  • Creators creditedOnly videos from creators who gave permission, tagged on every post.

How it works

  1. Permission first. A creator gives permission to repost clips of their videos, on the condition that they are tagged and credited.
  2. Clips are found. The app finds the highlight moments in a video and makes 30-second vertical (9:16) clips with captions and an on-screen credit.
  3. The owner reviews. Each clip waits in a review queue. The owner watches it, checks the caption and creator tag, and approves or rejects it.
  4. Approved clips are posted. The app uploads approved clips through the platforms' official APIs, such as TikTok's Content Posting API, with the creator's handle for that platform in the caption.

Platforms

The app connects only through official sign-in: TikTok Login Kit, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) login, and Google sign-in for YouTube. It asks only for the permissions needed to post, and the owner can disconnect at any time from each platform's settings. Login tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
