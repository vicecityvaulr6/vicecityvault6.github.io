Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · Last updated 18 November 2026

1. Who we are

vicecityvault6 clipper (the "App") is a personal software tool operated by the creator behind the vicecityvault6 social media accounts (the "Operator", "we", "us"). The App turns gaming videos into short vertical clips and, after the Operator reviews and approves each clip, posts it to social media accounts that the Operator owns and has connected: TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.

The App is not a public service. It has no sign-up and no user accounts, and other people cannot use it to post to their own accounts.

2. Information the App handles

From connected platforms (the Operator's own accounts)

When the Operator connects an account through the platform's official login (OAuth), the App receives:

PlatformInformation receivedWhy
TikTokAccess and refresh tokens, granted permissions (scopes), open ID, display name, and posting settings returned by the Content Posting API (allowed privacy levels, maximum video length)To upload approved clips to the Operator's TikTok account with valid settings
Instagram / Facebook (Meta)Access tokens, Facebook Page ID and name, linked Instagram account ID and username, publishing quotaTo publish approved Reels to the Operator's Page and Instagram account
YouTube (Google)Access and refresh tokens, channel ID and nameTo upload approved Shorts to the Operator's channel

The App does not request or read followers, messages, comments, likes, contacts, location, or any data about viewers or other platform users.

Created by the App

  • Video clips, captions and hashtags that the App generates for the Operator's review.
  • A local record of each post: platform, post ID, post link, time and status, used to prevent duplicate posts.
  • Local error logs, with tokens and secrets automatically removed.

About content creators

The App only clips videos from creators who have given the Operator permission to repost their content. For each creator it stores their public name, public YouTube channel and public social media handles (used to tag and credit them in every post) and a note of the permission they gave. Social media handles are taken only from links the creator publishes on their own YouTube channel, or supplied by the Operator.

3. How information is used

  • To upload clips that the Operator has approved to the Operator's own connected accounts.
  • To credit and tag the original creator correctly on each platform.
  • To check posting status, avoid duplicate posts, and keep a history of what was posted.

Information is not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models, and is not combined with data from other sources.

4. Storage and security

All information is stored on the Operator's own computer. Tokens and app secrets are kept in files readable only by the Operator's user account, are entered with hidden typing, and are removed from all logs and on-screen output. Tokens are sent only to the platform that issued them, over HTTPS. The App runs no web server and has no database in the cloud.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information. Information is sent only to the platform it belongs to (TikTok, Meta or Google) when the App calls that platform's official API to post a clip or check a post. Each platform handles that information under its own privacy policy: TikTok Privacy Policy, Meta Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy.

6. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the App can be removed at any time from Google account security settings.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected or the access expires, and are deleted when the Operator disconnects the account.
  • Posting records are kept so duplicate posts can be prevented, and can be deleted by the Operator at any time.
  • Clip files are deleted by the Operator after posting or rejection.

You can revoke the App's access at any time: in TikTok's Settings and privacy, under the security and permissions section (Apps and services); in Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations; and in your Google account under Third-party apps & services. A creator who wants their name, handles or clips removed, or wants to withdraw permission, can contact us (section 10); we will stop using their content and delete their details.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children and is used only by its adult Operator. We do not knowingly handle information from anyone under 13.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions, removal requests or permission withdrawals: send a direct message to @vicecityvault6 on Instagram.
